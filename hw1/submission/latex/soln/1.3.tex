The resulting dynamics are defined by
\begin{equation}
    \delta \dot{x} = 
    \begin{bmatrix}
        0 & 0 & \cos\theta^r & -v\sin\theta^r \\
        0 & 0 & \sin\theta^r & v\cos\theta^r \\
        0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\
        0 & 0 & 0 & 0
    \end{bmatrix} \delta x + 
    \begin{bmatrix}
        0 & 0 \\
        0 & 0 \\
        1 & 0 \\
        0 & 1
    \end{bmatrix} \delta u
\end{equation}
where $\delta x = x - x^r$, $x^r = [p_x^r,p_y^r,v^r,\theta^r]^T$, $\delta u = u - u^r$.
